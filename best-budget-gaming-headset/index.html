Best for specific needs

Best for wireless freedom on a budget

If you want to ditch the cable without breaking the bank, the Corsair HS65 Surround Wireless is the obvious choice. It offers reliable low-latency audio, a comfortable fit, and battery life that covers even the longest gaming sessions. It’s not the best for competitive shooters, but for casual and story-driven gaming it’s a fantastic all-rounder. Our pick: Corsair HS65 Surround Wireless.

Best for audiophiles on a budget

The HyperX Cloud Alpha S is the headset to get if pure sound quality is your top priority. It delivers a punchy, detailed soundstage that rivals headsets costing twice as much. Best for competitive multiplayer

When every footstep matters, the Razer BlackShark V2 HyperSpeed shines. Its lightweight design and treble-focused tuning let you pinpoint enemy positions with clarity. The wireless connection is fast and reliable, and the detachable mic keeps your voice clear under pressure. It’s the best pick for esports-minded players who still need to stick to a budget. Our pick: Razer BlackShark V2 HyperSpeed.

Our verdict

All three of these headsets prove you don’t need to spend a lot to get great gaming audio. For most people, the Corsair HS65 Surround Wireless offers the best balance of convenience and performance. But if you can live with a wire, the HyperX Cloud Alpha S gives you even better sound and comfort. And if you’re chasing that competitive edge, the Razer BlackShark V2 HyperSpeed is worth the small extra investment. Pick the one that matches your gaming style, and you’ll be set for 2026.
